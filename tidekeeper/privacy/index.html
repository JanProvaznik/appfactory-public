Tidekeeper privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026 (applies from version 0.2.0)

Tidekeeper predicts tides on your device using public data from NOAA that is bundled with the app. Tide data and your station choice never leave your device. The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob.

Advertising

To show ads, the app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may collect and process the following to serve, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud: your device's advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address, interactions with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. This data is sent to Google and its advertising partners, not to us. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time with Ad privacy settings at the bottom of the main screen. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Location

If you tap Nearest, the app asks for approximate location to sort tide stations by distance. This location is used only on your device and is not shared with advertisers by the app.

Data stored on your device

Your selected station, unit preference and consent choice are saved in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Children

Tidekeeper is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that collect or share data (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Tillfold privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Tillfold is an iPhone app that keeps your loyalty, membership and gift cards and shows their barcodes at the till. It works entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. Tillfold has no account, no analytics, no advertising and makes no network connections. The cards you add (store name, number, notes, balance, expiry date, colour) are stored only in the app's private storage on your iPhone.

Camera

If you scan a card, Tillfold uses the camera only to read the barcode, on your device. Camera images are not saved and never leave your iPhone.

Photos

If you add a card from a photo, you choose the photo in the system picker; Tillfold reads the barcode from that one image on your device and does not keep it. The app has no access to the rest of your library.

Notifications

If you turn on expiry reminders, Tillfold schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Backups

When you export a backup, Tillfold writes a JSON file to the location you choose in Files (for example iCloud Drive). That file is then handled by the service you chose, under its own terms. Tillfold only reads backup files you pick yourself.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Tillfold is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that collect or share data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Pixwright privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Pixwright is an iPhone app that converts, resizes, compresses, crops and removes metadata from images. All processing happens on your device.

Data we collect

None. Pixwright has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Your photos

Photos and files you choose are processed on your iPhone and never leave the device. Pixwright only sees the images you pick with the system photo picker or file browser. Processed copies are kept in temporary storage until you save or share them, and are deleted when you start over.

Photo library access

When you tap Save to Photos, the app asks for add-only access so it can save the processed images. It cannot read or browse your library.

Metadata

Pixwright can show and remove EXIF, GPS location, camera and IPTC information embedded in images. It reads this information only to display it to you or to remove it from the copies you export; it is not stored or sent anywhere.

Purchases

The optional Pixwright Pro unlock is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Pixwright is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that collect or share data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

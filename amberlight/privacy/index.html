Amberlight privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Amberlight calculates the sun's position, golden hour, blue hour, sunrise and sunset on your device with standard astronomical formulas.

Data we collect

None. Amberlight has no account, no analytics and does not request internet access.

Location

If you tap Use my location, the app asks for approximate location to calculate sun times for where you are. It is saved only in the app's private storage on your device and is never shared.

Children

Amberlight is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that collect or share data (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

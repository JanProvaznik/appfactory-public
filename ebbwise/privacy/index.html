Ebbwise privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Ebbwise is an offline flashcard app for iPhone. It imports deck files you choose, schedules reviews on your device and keeps everything on your iPhone.

Data we collect

None. Ebbwise has no account, no sync, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your decks and study history

Decks you import (.apkg or .colpkg files you open from Files or the share sheet), cards you create, images and sounds inside decks, and your review history are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded or shared. Deleting a deck or the app removes them. Your device's own backups (iCloud or computer backups, if you use them) may include this storage, as they do for any app.

Purchases

The optional Ebbwise Pro unlock is processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details; the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked.

Children

Ebbwise is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that collect or share data (for example sync), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

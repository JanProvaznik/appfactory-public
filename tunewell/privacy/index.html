Tunewell privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Tunewell is a tuner and metronome that works entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. Tunewell has no account, no analytics and does not request internet access.

Microphone

The tuner uses the microphone, only while the tuner screen is open, to measure the pitch of your instrument. Audio is analysed in memory on your device and is never recorded, stored or shared.

Children

Tunewell is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that collect or share data (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

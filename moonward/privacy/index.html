Moonward privacy policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Moonward calculates moon phases and rise/set times on your device with standard astronomical formulas.

Data we collect

None. Moonward has no account, no analytics and does not request internet access.

Location

If you tap Use my location, the app asks for approximate location to calculate moonrise and moonset for where you are. It is saved only in the app's private storage on your device and is never shared.

Children

Moonward is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that collect or share data (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
